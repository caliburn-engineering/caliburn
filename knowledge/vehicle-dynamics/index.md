## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Pacejka Magic Formula | Semi-empirical tyre model mapping slip to force |
| Brake blending | Splitting braking between regen and friction |
| ECMS | Equivalent Consumption Minimisation Strategy |
| Skyhook | Semi-active damping relative to inertial frame |
| Torque fill | E-motor fills ICE torque gap during shifts |
| Drivetrain shuffle | Torsional oscillation from sudden torque |
| Derating | Reducing power output due to thermal limits |
